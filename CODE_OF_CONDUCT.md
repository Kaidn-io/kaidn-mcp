# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue with the code, not the person.

## Expected

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason attached.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way.
- Respect that maintainers say no to things that are fine but out of scope.
- Remember that people here have different first languages, timezones and
  levels of experience.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

Applies in this repository — issues, pull requests, discussions — and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@kaidn.io**. Reports are handled
privately, and we will not discuss them publicly.

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that breach this, and may
block accounts for repeated or severe breaches.
